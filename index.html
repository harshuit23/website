About ErinGlen Wellness

Our internationally trained Physiotehrapist spent a decade working as a physiotherapy aide in Canadian clinics before pursuing certification as a registered massage therapist. This extensive background provides a strong foundation in functional anatomy and rehabilitative care. Dedicated to improving the quality of life for every client, our therapist focuses on delivering evidence-based, personalized treatment plans. Each session begins with a comprehensive understanding of the client’s unique needs, goals, and preferences. Treatment is then tailored to relieve pain, restore function, and support long-term well-being and health..

We believe that true wellness comes from balancing body, mind, and spirit. Every session is tailored to your individual needs, ensuring you receive the most beneficial treatment possible.
